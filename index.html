About

I am a postdoctoral research fellow at Visual Geometry Group, University of Oxford, working with Prof. Andrea Vedaldi. I completed my PhD in September 2025, at Computer Vision Lab, EPFL, supervised by Prof. Mathieu Salzmann and Prof. Pascal Fua.

My research interests lie in 3D computer vision, with a specific focus on Spatial AI and Embodied AI.
